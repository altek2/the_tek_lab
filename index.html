Team Members


Who Are We?
We are a group of highly enthusiastic, motivated scientists who believe in the power of genetics and agriculture with a focus on chromosomes. We are in different academic levels of experience such as undergraduates, masters, and doctorate students along with our mentor. We are a dynamic group with different interests, personalities, gender, and culture. We believe that our research experience in the lab is unique for our personal, scientific, and professional development. The support among the lab members at all levels, one-for-all, makes us achieve challenges in plant chromosome research to become the scientists of the future.


Welcome to the wonderful world of chromosomes!


​Ahmet Latif Tek
Professor
​
​​Ahmet graduated from Agricultural Engineering, Ankara. He finished his MSc and PhD in Plant Breeding and Plant Genetics, University of Wisconsin, Madison. He was awarded a JSPS fellowship in Okayama University, Japan. He worked in Field Crops, Agriculture Faculty, Harran University, Şanlıurfa, Turkey. Now, he has a faculty member in Agricultural Genetic Engineering, Ayhan Şahenk Faculty of Agricultural Sciences and Technologies, Niğde, Turkey. 


​​Hümeyra Yıldız Akkamış
Postdoctoral Researcher

Hümeyra graduated from Molecular Biology and Genetic, İstanbul University. She finished her MSc and PhD in Agricultural Genetic Engineering, Niğde Ömer Halisdemir University, Niğde, Turkey. She is currently working as a postdoctoral researcher in a TÜBİTAK 1001 project on characterization of endogenous pararetrovirus (EPRV) in legumes.
